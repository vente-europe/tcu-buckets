Key findings - basis for the offer

Looking at the last 12 months of EU sales velocity (May 2025 – Apr 2026, full year including BFCM + Christmas), the stock splits cleanly into 3 buckets with very different recovery profiles:

  • FAST stock - at current velocity Mothership would recover ~97% of COGS within 12 months if they sold it themselves
  • SLOW stock - only ~10% of units would sell in 12 months (≈13.5% of SLOW COGS recovered); the rest takes 24-36+ months
  • DEAD stock - 0% recovery: zero EU sales across the entire year despite Black Friday and Christmas peaks

Therefore I propose 3 different buckets with 3 distinct discount levels - 80% / 50% / 30% of COGS respectively - to reflect the very different value each bucket represents. The discounts are calibrated against Mothership's realistic 12-month recovery, with logic explained per bucket below. All of this is offered as full upfront cash payment for the entire stock in one go - zero installments, zero security collateral, zero operational handover delays.
