How this calculator works (logic)

3-bucket model based on each ASIN's 12-month EU velocity (May 2025 – Apr 2026, DE+FR+IT+ES summed - full-year window covers BFCM + Christmas + New Year peak):

  • FAST (≥30 units/month avg): self-selling. Mothership would recover ~94% COGS within 12 months. Pay FAST × COGS (default 0.80) - discount = time-value of cash-now vs. spread over 4-12 months.
  • SLOW (1–30 units/month avg): tail-heavy. In 12 months only ~10.8% of units sell (~13.5% of SLOW COGS recovered) - the rest dribbles over 24-36+ months. Pay SLOW × COGS (default 0.50) - Mothership still gets decent money up-front, you absorb the entire long tail + FBA storage burden + risk of SLOW→DEAD drift.
  • DEAD (0 units/month across FULL 12mo): structurally dead - zero demand even during BFCM + Christmas peak. Pay DEAD × COGS (default 0.30 = 30% of COGS) - scrap-like value. Mothership can't sell these without PPC + listing rebuild + zero ROI guarantee. You're buying their liability, not their asset.

COGS per ASIN: pre-filled in the table from historical P&L (3-year sales window). Each ASIN has its own COGS (e.g. Bungee 45cm ~£1.14, Garden Tool Belt ~£2.40). Editable per row - adjust as Mothership shares actual landed costs.

Why the discount on FAST: Mothership would recover ~91% of FAST COGS in 6 months and 94% in 12 months at current velocity - but spread out, with capital tied up, FBA storage cost, and risk of velocity drop. Paying ~80% of each ASIN's COGS now compensates them fairly while delivering certainty + speed.
